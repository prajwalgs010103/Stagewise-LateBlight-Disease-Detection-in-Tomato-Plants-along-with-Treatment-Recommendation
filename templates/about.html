About Our Project

Welcome to the Tomato Leaf Disease Detector, an innovative web application that leverages artificial intelligence and deep learning to revolutionize plant healthcare. Our mission is to empower farmers, gardeners, and agricultural enthusiasts with cutting-edge technology to protect their crops.

AI-Powered Detection

Our advanced machine learning models analyze tomato plant leaf images with remarkable accuracy, identifying disease stages that might be invisible to the naked eye.

Real-Time Analysis

Get instant results with our efficient processing system, allowing for quick decision-making to protect your plants before damage becomes irreversible.

Actionable Insights

We don't just identify problems - we provide detailed treatment recommendations tailored to each specific disease stage and plant condition.

How It Works

The system processes uploaded images of tomato plant leaves through our trained deep learning models, which have been trained on thousands of images to recognize various disease patterns and progression stages.

Key Features

  • Comprehensive Disease Detection: Identifies multiple common tomato plant diseases including early blight, late blight, leaf mold, and more.
  • Stage-Specific Recommendations: Provides different treatment plans based on disease progression stage.
  • User-Friendly Interface: Designed specifically for farmers and gardening enthusiasts with minimal technical knowledge.
  • Historical Tracking: Maintains a record of all your detections for future reference and progress tracking.

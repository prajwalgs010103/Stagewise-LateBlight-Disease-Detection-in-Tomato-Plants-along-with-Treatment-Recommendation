Tomato Leaf Lateblight Disease Stage Detector

Upload an image of a tomato leaf to detect its lateblight disease stage and receive treatment recommendations.

Tips for Best Results

  • Upload only tomato leaf image
  • Don't upload any other images
  • Use clear, well-lit photos of individual leaves
  • Capture the leaf against a plain background if possible
  • Make sure the entire leaf is visible in the image
  • For early detection, photograph leaves showing the first signs of discoloration

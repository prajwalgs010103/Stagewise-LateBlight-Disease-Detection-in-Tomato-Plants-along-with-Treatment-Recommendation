Necessary Precautions

Tomato plants are prone to diseases mostly in the rainy season, so the below list of precautions must be followed without fail during this period. However, in the summer season, missing some precautions won't have a significant impact—just the necessary medications can be applied when the disease shows up.

Benevia - 360ml per acre

Insecticide which should be given to the plants within the first 10 days after planting the saplings. 360 ml of Benevia should be used for an acre of land.

Second round of Benevia should be given in the next 10 days (within 20 days after planting the saplings). Again, 360 ml of Benevia should be used for an acre of land.

MC Set - 2ml per litre of water

Plant growth promoter which should be given in the next 10 days (within 30 days after planting the saplings) but not together with Abamectin.

Abamectin - 1ml per litre of water

A chemical compound used as an insecticide and anthelmintic, which should be given within 30 days after planting the saplings but not together with MC Set.

Ridomil Gold + Isobian + Coragen

These 3 chemicals should be applied together in the next 10 days (within 40 days after planting the saplings).

  • Ridomil Gold (1-1.5ml per litre of water)
  • Isobian (2-3ml per litre of water)
  • Corazin (0.4ml per litre of water)

Equation Pro or Kocide 2000

Fungicides that should be applied in the next 10 days (within 50 days after planting the saplings). It helps prevent late blight and other diseases.

  • Equation Pro (1ml per litre of water)
  • Kocide 2000 (2g per litre of water)

Acrobat or Polyram

Fungicides that should be applied in the next 10 days (within 60 days after planting the saplings). It effectively treats late blight and other diseases.

  • Acrobat (1g per litre of water)
  • Polyram (4g per litre of water)

Delegate - 180ml per acre

Insecticide to be given during the fruiting (Hooja) stage in the specified quantity to avoid insects and protect the crop.

Treatment for Lateblight Disease Stages

Stage 1

Cosuit - 3g per litre of water

Fungicide should be given in the specified quantity to treat the Lateblight disease in an early stage.

Stage 2

Equation Pro (1ml per litre of water) or Kocide 2000 (2g per litre of water)

Fungicides that should be applied within 50 days after planting the saplings. It takes care of Lateblight disease in the middle stages.

Stage 3

Acrobat (1g per litre of water) or Polyram (4g per litre)

Fungicides that should be applied within 60 days after planting the saplings. It takes care of Lateblight disease in the last stages for sure.

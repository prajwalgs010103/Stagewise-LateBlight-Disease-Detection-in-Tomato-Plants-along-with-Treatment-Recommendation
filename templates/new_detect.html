Tomato Leaf Disease Detector

Upload multiple images of only tomato leaves to detect diseases and if the detected disease is Lateblight then you can proceed to check it's stage.

How to Use

  1. Upload only leaf images
  2. Don't upload any other images
  3. Either upload images from your device or use the camera to capture new ones
  4. You can select multiple images at once (JPG, PNG formats)
  5. For best results, use clear, well-lit photos of individual leaves
  6. Click "Detect" to analyze all uploaded images
